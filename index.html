The Problem

Sound familiar?

Many Western North Carolina manufacturers face recurring challenges filling skilled roles, developing future talent, and navigating a workforce landscape that includes multiple programs, providers, and possible resources.

Atlas was built to help employers define the problem clearly and develop a practical path forward.

  • 📋 Hard-to-fill roles stay open longer than your operation can afford
  • 🚪 Hiring and retention problems keep repeating without a long-term pipeline
  • ⏳ Experienced workers retire and critical knowledge becomes harder to replace
  • 🗺️ Workforce, training, apprenticeship, and funding options can be difficult to navigate
How It Works

Atlas helps turn a workforce problem into an actionable plan

We start with the employer's need, map realistic options, and help coordinate next steps where appropriate.

1

Workforce Assessment

We learn your operation, recurring hiring challenges, priority occupations, required skills, and timeline.

2

Pipeline Strategy

We define what a sustainable candidate pipeline should look like and identify possible sourcing channels based on your needs.

3

Training & Resource Mapping

When a skills gap exists, we identify relevant education, training, apprenticeship, and workforce-development resources for the employer to evaluate.

4

Coordination & Follow-Through

When an employer chooses to move forward, Atlas can help organize introductions, responsibilities, milestones, and outcome tracking.

Meet the Founder

Allen Walker

Founder · Atlas Workforce Solutions LLC

For 30 years, Allen Walker walked the streets of West Asheville as a U.S. Postal Service letter carrier — showing up every day, in every neighborhood, building trust with businesses and communities across the region.

Over three decades of working throughout the community, he developed a close view of the region's employers, neighborhoods, and changing workforce needs.

That experience shaped his interest in helping local employers build clearer, more sustainable workforce strategies.

"When you spend 30 years showing up for a community, you start to feel a responsibility to it. That's why I founded Atlas."

Atlas was built on a simple belief: when manufacturers and workers connect well, the whole region wins.

The Workforce Ecosystem

One employer need. Multiple possible resources.

Western North Carolina includes public workforce programs, education and training providers, apprenticeship resources, economic-development organizations, and other community assets. Atlas helps employers understand which types of resources may be relevant and facilitates outreach only when appropriate.

Public Workforce Resources

Potential recruiting, training, and employer-support resources administered by public workforce organizations.

Education & Training Providers

Colleges, training providers, and continuing-education programs that employers may evaluate for skill development.

Apprenticeship Resources

Public and private resources that can help employers understand apprenticeship models, requirements, and registration pathways.

Economic Development Resources

Organizations and programs that may support regional business growth, expansion, and workforce strategy.

Industry & Community Resources

Associations, nonprofits, and local initiatives that may contribute expertise, referrals, or project support.

Employer-Led Coordination

Atlas keeps the employer's need at the center and helps organize outreach without representing any third party.
